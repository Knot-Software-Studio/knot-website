# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
- Primary audience: Discerning mobile users, friend groups, and trip organizers looking for thoughtful, privacy-first event coordination, discovery, and shared planning.
- Secondary audience: Potential beta testers, early adopters, and collaborators interested in Knot Studio's vision and upcoming product lineup.

## Product Purpose
- The website is the digital front door and flagship portfolio for **Knot**, an independent software studio based in Austria.
- It articulates the studio's craft philosophy and showcases active projects, headlined by Knot's mobile Event Planner (iOS & Android) featuring interactive map traversal, swipe-based discovery, personal accounts, calendar integration, and shared planning tools.
- Success means communicating studio caliber, establishing anticipation for the studio's vision, and capturing high-intent waitlist signups for upcoming beta testing waves.

## Positioning
- An independent studio crafting soulful, privacy-first software with relentless attention to tactile motion and visual polish.
- Knot combines the refined restraint of Apple Human Interface design with Material 3 Expressive fluid dynamics, standing in stark contrast to engagement-seeking, ad-monetized utilities.

## Operating Context
- Web showcase accessed seamlessly across desktop and mobile devices.
- Gateway to upcoming mobile app releases on iOS (TestFlight / App Store) and Android (Google Play Beta).
- Touchpoints: Direct waitlist registration form, direct founder email channels (`office@knot-software.com`, `support@knot-software.com`).

## Capabilities and Constraints
- **Studio Showcase**: Hero narrative, studio vision, active project pipeline, and philosophy bento.
- **Flagship Project (Event Planner App)**: Highlight core mobile features:
  - Event creation & date coordination
  - Interactive map traversal & Tinder-style swipe discovery
  - Personal accounts & integrated calendar
  - Shared group planning (polls, tasks, photos, expense splitting)
- **Early Access / Beta Capture**: Responsive waitlist registration and feedback touchpoints.
- **Constraints**: Pure web frontend (React + Vite), fast static delivery, zero tracking bloat.

## Brand Commitments
- **Name**: Knot (Knot Software Studio)
- **Origin**: Austria-based indie software studio
- **Aesthetic**: Apple-level elegance, Material 3 expressive fluidity, dark modern materials, tactile micro-interactions
- **Values**: Privacy-first (no ad tracking, no profiling), sub-100ms responsiveness, craftsmanship over engagement hacking
- **Tone & Voice**: Confident, refined, genuine, German/English bilingual readiness

## Evidence on Hand
- Interactive planner prototype and module demos in codebase (`src/components/Product.jsx`, `src/components/InteractivePlanner.jsx`)
- Direct contact endpoints: `office@knot-software.com`, `support@knot-software.com`
- Incumbent brand assets, glassmorphism tokens, and layout structure

## Product Principles
1. **Soulful Craft Over Engagement Hacking**: Tools designed to get people off screens and into real-life moments together.
2. **Absolute Privacy by Default**: Zero ad trackers, zero profile harvesting, complete respect for user data.
3. **Tactile Fluidity & Expressiveness**: Physics-driven micro-interactions, seamless transitions, sub-100ms feedback.
4. **Transparent Studio Identity**: Honest communication, direct access to founders, and open roadmap previews.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- High-contrast visual hierarchy, WCAG AA compliance for legibility on dark glass surfaces, fluid responsiveness across all viewport sizes, support for reduced motion preferences (`prefers-reduced-motion`).
