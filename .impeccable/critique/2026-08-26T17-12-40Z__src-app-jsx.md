### Design Specificity Verdict
**PASS**: The interface is uniquely grounded in Knot Studio's Austrian indie identity and its flagship mobile Event Planner. The design replaces marketing rhetoric with a working 4-mode mobile device canvas (Map traversal, Swipe deck, Calendar, Group tools). The deterministic detector scan confirmed **0 core slop antipatterns**, **0 layout animations**, and **0 gradient text instances**.

### Overall Impression
Tactile, confident, and deeply grounded. The Obsidian dark theme paired with Austrian terracotta accents communicates craftsmanship without falling into generic AI SaaS tropes.

### What's Working
1. **Interactive Device Proof Canvas**: Replaces static screenshots with a live 4-view simulator that lets visitors interact with map pins, swipe event ideas, check day timelines, and calculate expense splits.
2. **Cohesive Obsidian & Material 3 Fluidity**: Decelerated ease-out curves (`cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)`), 1px specular edges, and Manrope typography deliver high-end tactile feel.
3. **Respectful Conversion Flow**: Clean platform toggle (iOS / Android), instant toast feedback, and direct founder channels.

### Priority Issues
- **[P1] Direct Touch & Pointer Drag on Swipe Deck**: The swipe deck triggers card advances via buttons, but direct pointer/touch drag gestures would fulfill the tactile physical promise of the swipe metaphor. (Suggested command: `$impeccable delight` / `$impeccable animate`)
- **[P2] Viewport Height Scaling on Compact Mobile**: Ensure the phone chassis scales proportionally on compact mobile screens (`< 375px`) with `touch-action: pan-y` to avoid nested scrolling friction. (Suggested command: `$impeccable adapt`)
- **[P2] Micro-Typography Legibility Floor**: Several simulator micro-labels sit at `0.65rem` (10.4px); elevating to `0.72rem` (11.5px) ensures crisp legibility across all mobile displays. (Suggested command: `$impeccable typeset`)
- **[P3] Frontmatter Typography Synchronization**: Document `JetBrains Mono` in the `DESIGN.md` YAML frontmatter alongside `Manrope`. (Suggested command: `$impeccable document`)

### Persona Red Flags
- **Alex (Power User)**: Looking for technical sync standards (e.g. CalDAV / .ics export) and Austrian ISO server hosting details.
- **Jordan (First-Timer)**: Needs explicit reassurance that invited friends can vote via zero-install web links without downloading the mobile app.
- **Casey (Mobile Smartphone User)**: Requires `touch-action: pan-y` on simulator sub-elements so map interaction never traps vertical page scrolling.

### Minor Observations & Questions to Consider
- Dark frosted glass Sonner toast system delivers an elegant tactile feedback loop.
- *Question to consider*: What if visitors could drop their own interactive pin anywhere on the Austrian map to immediately simulate creating an outing?
